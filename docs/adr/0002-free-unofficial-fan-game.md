# Free, unofficial, non-commercial fan game

Dragonsteel Entertainment permits non-commercial fan works, forbids crowdfunding, and does not explicitly address fan video games; Sanderson is also negotiating a licensed AAA Mistborn game. We build this as a free fan game with no monetisation or donations, clearly labelled unofficial, with "Mistborn" never the lead word of the title or any storefront or studio name. In exchange we take total fidelity to the books rather than keeping names and story reskinnable as original IP. Seeking a licence first was rejected as unrealistic during the AAA negotiations.
