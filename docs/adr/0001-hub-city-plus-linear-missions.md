# Hub city plus linear story missions

The game must follow the trilogy's fixed plot closely while making Steelpush traversal the centrepiece. We structure it as an explorable night-time Luthadel hub between hand-authored linear story missions, each adapting one or two chapters. A metroidvania was rejected because Vin has all eight basic metals by chapter 7 (no natural ability gating), the plot is fixed, and the story leaves Luthadel repeatedly; missions-only was rejected because corridors reduce blue lines to grapple points. Consequence: two level-design languages (open hub, authored mission) and story-trigger gating to stop hub exploration breaking sequence.
