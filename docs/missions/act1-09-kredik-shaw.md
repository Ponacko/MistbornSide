# Mission 9: Kredik Shaw

Adapts The Final Empire, chapter 14 (Vin and Kelsier infiltrate Kredik Shaw, fight guards, open the hut within the chamber, meet Inquisitor Bendal; Vin takes the logbook, is wounded by an obsidian axe, flees across the city hunted by the Inquisitor, and collapses at Sazed's feet). Vertical slice mission. Decisions recorded 2026-10-03 during the design interview; open items are marked.

## Role in the game
The first real infiltration and the game's statement about Inquisitors: you do not fight them yet. Shows stealth (tin and copper), guard combat with armour Pushes, the oppressive Soothing of the palace, atium's first use in danger, and the chase as a traversal set piece. The axe wound ends the mission with Vin losing.

## Shape (from the book)
1. Approach and entry: the spires exterior at night, a way in past the outer guards.
2. Interior with Kelsier: corridors and halls; guard fights where armour makes Pushes a weapon; the Lord Ruler's Soothing pressing on Vin.
3. The domed mural chamber and the hut within it. Kelsier opens the hut.
4. Bendal: the unwinnable encounter (see DESIGN.md 10.4). Vin burns atium, takes the logbook, is struck by the obsidian axe.
5. The chase: Vin, wounded, flees across the roofs of Luthadel with Bendal coin-jumping behind her. Ends when she collapses; Sazed's carriage; cut to black.

## Kelsier's role (decided)
Invulnerable melee companion with simple AI through the interior: follows through corridors, engages the nearest guard with dagger and shove, cannot die, never Pushes off into traversal. The same companion system serves Ham and Breeze call-ins later.

## Chase (decided)
An authored rooftop corridor with several valid lines (coins, brackets, a gate) and Bendal as a live pursuer who coin-jumps behind Vin and closes if she stalls. Death restarts at a recent roof. Ends at a scripted collapse point.

## The wound (decided)
During the chase Vin's health is pinned to a sliver that cannot regenerate; pewter is locked on and drains visibly. Running out of pewter before the collapse point counts as a death and restarts the chase. Screen edges darken with each hit.

## Stealth versus combat (decided)
The spire exterior and entry are a stealth section: tin, copper, patrols, a Seeker obligator. Once inside with Kelsier, the guard fights are unavoidable combat arenas, as in the chapter.
