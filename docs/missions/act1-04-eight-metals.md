# Mission 4: Eight Metals

Adapts The Final Empire, chapter 7 (Kelsier gives Vin her mistcloak and teaches the eight basic metals on the rooftops of Luthadel; ends atop the city wall). Vertical slice mission. Decisions recorded 2026-10-03 during the design interview; open items are marked.

## Role in the game
The Steelsight tutorial and the first time the player controls Vin with Allomancy. The player has already flown as Kelsier in mission 1, so this mission's job is to make Vin's own first Push feel earned, and to teach the weight rule through the arrows rather than text.

## Lesson order (decided)
Book order: tin, then pewter, then steel and iron. Copper, bronze, zinc and brass are explained by Kelsier in dialogue during the mission and practised in later missions (Clubs, Marsh, Breeze).

## Kelsier's presence (decided)
Scripted demonstrator. At each lesson beat Kelsier performs the move once as a scripted animation, then waits as a silhouette in the mists at the next vantage point. No companion AI.

## Toggles (decided)
At the tin and pewter beats Kelsier names the metal, a key prompt appears, and nothing advances until the player presses the toggle. Later beats never prompt again.

## Length (decided)
8 to 12 minutes on a first playthrough; replays under 3 minutes.

## Beats (draft)
1. Clubs' shop, night. Kelsier hands Vin the mistcloak (cutscene; cloak chain sim starts). Vin drinks her first vial: eight reserves appear in the HUD one by one.
2. Rooftop above the shop. Tin: the mists thin, the city reveals itself, a patrol's footsteps ring through the fog. Kelsier warns about light; a lamplighter below demonstrates the whiteout.
3. Roof gaps. Pewter: Vin cannot make the first gap without it; with it she can. Kelsier explains the crash in one line.
4. The coin. Kelsier drops a clip, Pushes, and lands on a higher roof. Vin copies him: drop, Push. The anchor cone is taught here by a gap that requires standing over the coin.
5. Pull. A lamp bracket across a street; Vin Pulls herself to it. Then Pulling a coin back to her hand.
6. The run to the wall. A rooftop line with coins, brackets and a gate, ending with the climb up the city wall. Kelsier is ahead, visible as a silhouette in the mists.
7. Atop the wall (end of mission; chapter 8 begins mission 5).
