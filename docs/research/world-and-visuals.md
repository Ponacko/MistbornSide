# Research: World, Visuals, Costumes, Adaptations

Supporting research for `docs/DESIGN.md`. Compiled 2026-10-02 from Coppermind, Brandon Sanderson's sample chapters and annotations, Reactor, Shire Post, Dragonsteel, and fan-art and fan-game sources. Fan-derived details are flagged.

## 1. The look of Scadrial

Sky and sun: ash in the upper atmosphere makes the sun red and the light crimson or golden. Prologue: "the ruddy, mid-day sky", a sun of "brilliant crimson red" behind "the smoky blackness of the upper sky". Sanderson's own palette summary: "brown vegetation and a red sky". Nights are near-starless; the mists hide the stars, but a Tineye can see them.

Ash: falls from seven Ashmounts (Tyrian, Zerinah, Doriel, Faleast, Morag, Kalling, Torinost). It is explicitly black. Opening line: "Ash fell from the sky." "The puffs of soot fell like black snowflakes." City staining: "darkest at the top, where the ash gathered, but rainwaters and evening condensations had carried the stains over ledges and down walls. Like paint running down a canvas, the darkness seemed to creep down the sides of buildings in an uneven gradient." Streets completely black. Sprite cue: every structure gets a black top gradient bleeding downward, plus a black ground layer.

Plants: brown or yellow-brown; no flowers exist; the only flower is Mare's picture. Hardy scrub, small aspen forests (aspen bark resists ash stains, so Fellise lines streets with them). Surviving animals: horses, dogs, goats, sheep.

The mists: appear shortly after sunset every night, simply fade into existence, not fog. Faintly luminescent, making the night a little less dark. They distort sound, will not enter buildings or tents: "Thick mist immediately began to pour through the portal, drifting lazily across Kelsier's body, pooling on the floor and creeping across the dirt like a hesitant animal." "High noble keeps became ghostly, looming silhouettes. Streets seemed to grow more narrow in the fog, every thoroughfare becoming a lonely, dangerous alleyway." Skaa fear them; nobles and Allomancers do not.

The Deepness: canonically the mists lingering into day under Ruin's influence. In Ministry art it is a violet formless mass (Keep Venture windows). A canonical black mist is Ruin's Investiture, seen around the Well as it fills and around Ruin. Red mists are not canon; the only red is sunlight through ash.

Book 3 decay: "Black ash fell lightly from the sky, as it did most days lately"; land "looked as if it were blanketed in deep black snow"; "blood-red sun". Ashmounts emit massively more ash and lava; lava rivers block roads; earthquakes devour cities. The mists stay later into the morning, come during the day, and sicken people (16% Snap). The mists recoil from Hemalurgy: anyone with a spike slightly repulses them; koloss feel hatred from them. In the finale the mists flow to Vin from across the planet and visibly leak from her body (like Stormlight, per Sanderson); she levels Kredik Shaw, leaving a crater.

Restored world: blue sky, clouds, a yellow sun, flowers, green plants. Spook sees grass and a blue sky for the first time; Vin and Elend lie in a field of flowers recreated from Mare's picture.

## 2. Luthadel architecture

Scale: roughly two million inhabitants (Word of Brandon). The only walled city; eight gates named for metals (on the map, not in prose). Known gate positions from the siege: Tin (N), Pewter (NE), Zinc (E), Steel (NW, across the River Channerel). Districts on the map: The Twists, Ashwarrens, Sootwarrens, Aspen Row, Hotel District, Old Gate, Commercial District, The Cracks, Southbridge, Brassgate, Industrial District, Blockstreet, Grainfield; Kenton Street shopping district. Fourwell Crossroads with a copper statue of the Lord Ruler is fan-wiki flavour.

Building stock: "stone blocks, with tile roofs for the wealthy, and simple, peaked wooden roofs for the rest", packed closely, generally three stories. Skaa tenements line dark streets; sick skaa huddle in gutters with soot drifting around them. Hundreds of forges, refineries and mills add smoke. Cobblestone streets; canals (Luth-Davn Canal) connect to the river. Fellise by contrast is whitewashed granite with aspen-lined streets.

Keeps: ten Great House keeps, monolithic, with rows of spear-like spires or deep archways, modelled on Khlenni cathedrals (Sanderson used the Washington National Cathedral for Venture). Lit at night by limelights with mirrors.
- Keep Venture: massive, rectangular; eight limelights outside. Main hall four or five stories high, several times as long as wide. Enormous rectangular stained-glass windows in rows, lit from outside, throwing cascades of colour. Floor tiles in a curling grey and white mist pattern; pillars topped with carved animals; a single-story gallery; banners from a buttressed arched ceiling; an upper balcony lit by small lanterns of blue glass in wrought-iron vine brackets. Windows depict the Lord Ruler and the violet Deepness.
- Keep Hasting: a tall wide central tower with six satellite towers joined by wall-top walkways, all covered in curling stained glass; ballroom at the top reached by pulley platforms. Just west of the Channerel.
- Keep Elariel: squat single-story ballroom wing; stained glass in the ceiling as circular rose-window skylights lit from the roof; sunken dance floor; two tiers of candle-lit tables; secret alcoves.
- Keep Lekal: based on the Luxor pyramid.

Kredik Shaw: Hill of a Thousand Spires, centre of the city between the Tin and Copper Gates, built over the Well. "Loomed like some kind of massive, multi-spined insect"; "an asymmetric collection of wings, towers and enormous black spires, straight or twisted, needle-like or wide"; spires from room-sized towers to thin rods of iron; no outer wall; several times the size of any keep. Standing near it feels like constant Soothing. Throne room: a cylindrical chamber whose single curved wall is coloured glass with no specific scene, "the colors blown and melded together in long, thin trails, like mist". Beneath: a stalactite cavern and the Well, a silvery white glowing pool about five yards across that beats "Thump. Thump. Thump."

Civic landmarks: Fountain Square (enormous, central patio raised five feet with four bowl fountains; executions; near Clubs' shop on the road from the Steel Gate to Kredik Shaw). Luthadel Garrison (20,000 troops; fortress near the Steel Gate with spiked wall; soldiers in breastplate, cap and black cloak). Canton of Finance (tall, blocky, massive rose window, two red banners; austere grey interior). Conventical of Seran (Inquisition fortress in a crater to the south).

Other cities: Urteau (drained canals as streetslots, ash-filled troughs with ladders and ramps; the Marketpit; mostly wooden buildings; noble quarter with stained glass). Fadrex City (ringed by dark red and orange rock shelves; northern buildings with a fluid organic shape).

## 3. Costume and character design

Vin: barely over five feet, slight, black hair boyishly short in book 1, grown toward shoulder length later; one small earring. Street clothes: white buttoned shirt and overalls; later brown trousers and an untucked shirt. Night clothes: black trousers and a man's buttoning shirt, mistcloak, hood down. Paired obsidian daggers, coin pouch, vials on a sash. Elend: "some strange mixture of a noblewoman, a street urchin, and a cat." As Valette: first gown light blue with white ruffles and sapphire barrettes; red at Keep Elariel; maroon in WoA.

Mistcloak (Sanderson's spec): all dark charcoal grey; strips half an inch to an inch wide, cotton weight, each sewn individually, lengths within an inch or two, cut to obscure the feet without dragging; one and a half layers of tassels; slit from the rib cage down; tears free if grabbed. "It's colored and constructed to hide you in the mist... And it warns city guards and other Mistborn not to bother you." Fan art fans the tassels wide on a jump like wings.

Kelsier: tall, hawk-faced, light blond hair, hazel eyes, mid-thirties; layers of thin lengthwise scars on his arms; perpetual smile; nobleman's suit with coloured vest, mistcloak at night.

Elend: curly brown hair deliberately dishevelled; loose suits, buttons undone, always a book. As king/emperor: short hair, short beard, wooden crown, the white uniform trimmed in gold with cape and epaulets, white gloves, dueling cane; in battle soiled with ash and blood.

Sazed: tall, long-limbed, shaved head, stretched earlobes studded with earrings, Terris robes of embroidered overlapping V shapes in the master's house colours; metalminds as earrings, bracelets, rings; coppermind bracelets on forearms. Tindwyl: tall, lean, about fifty, waist-length greying auburn hair, alternating-colour earrings.

Steel Inquisitors: tall and lean, a head above obligators; bald; eye tattoos across the skull; two thick steel spikes through the eyes protruding several inches from the back of the head; 9 to 11 spikes; dark robes; distinctive posture; weep blood. Inquisition members add a stark red line on the side of the face. No canonical hooks; Ben McSweeney's concept gives them obsidian axes. Marsh: over six feet, hard square face; after conversion, scars mixed with faded tattoos.

Obligators: shaved heads, dark grey robes, intricate black tattoos ringing the eyes; complexity equals rank. Yomen: youthful, round-faced, an atium bead lodged in his forehead on a diadem.

Koloss: blue skin, beady red eyes; 5 to 12 ft; small ones with loose, folded, drooping skin; around 10 ft it tears, so the ancient are covered in scars and wrap themselves in leather; four iron spikes; swords their own size, blunt as clubs, nicked and jagged; pouches of stolen coins.

Kandra and mistwraiths: natural form a mass of translucent muscles like large snails; mistwraiths wear scavenged animal bones. TenSoon as a large wolfhound, later a horse; True Bodies of stone, wood, glass or crystal under translucent skin.

Lord Ruler: tall, handsome, pale, black hair, looks mid-twenties; black and white exaggerated nobleman's suit with a coat trailing to the floor and a pure black vest with brilliant white markings; rings as metalminds; atium bracelets on his upper arms piercing the skin.

Crew: Breeze (portly, slicked black hair, moustache, plum silk vest, black overcoat, short-brimmed hat, dueling cane). Ham (very short hair, sleeveless shirt and vest, muscled). Clubs (gnarled face like knotted wood, limp). Spook (gangly fifteen-year-old with Eastern street slang; later six feet plus, blindfolded, bearded). Dockson (stout, black hair, half-beard). Zane (medium height, wiry, narrow face, curly brown hair, all black, no mistcloak). Cett (paralysed from the waist down, carried in a chair). Allrianne (plump, long golden hair, pink or red dresses). Quellion's officials in bright red.

Skaa vs nobles: skaa in black, grey or brown smocks, roughspun trousers, leather vests, soot-stained coats. Nobles in white shirt, deep green vest with gold buttons, long black coat; dueling canes.

## 4. Metals, coins, props

- Boxing: gold imperial coin with Kredik Shaw on the back; 3,000 boxings is a fortune. Clip: small copper coin, Coinshot ammunition. Shire Post replicas by Isaac Stewart and Ben McSweeney: clip shows the Lord Ruler, boxing shows Kredik Shaw; about 2 cm and 3 cm.
- Metal vials: small glass vials of metal flakes in alcohol, worn on a belt or chest sash.
- Glass daggers: obsidian, in pairs. Dueling canes: about 2 ft of hardwood; hazekillers pair them with wooden shields. Obsidian axes for Inquisitors.
- Atium: "a tiny, silvery ball of metal. It was so reflective and bright that it almost seemed to be a drop of liquid, yet it was solid to the touch."
- Hemalurgic spikes: round, driven point-first; Inquisitor spikes thick and several inches long; kandra Blessings slender, under three inches. Any pointed metal qualifies (Vin's earring).
- Blue lines (book): "a multitude of faint blue lines sprung from her chest, streaking out into the spinning mists. Most of the lines were thin, like translucent pieces of twine, though a couple were as thick as yarn." Birthright's E3 demo used a thin blue line to pushable objects; fan projects use volumetric blue lines from the chest.

## 5. Soundscape and mood
Nights: silence, dripping condensation, mist-wetted cobblestones, mists that distort sounds. Days oppressive and dreary, ash whispering down. Balls: a string orchestra on a platform; servants in white; coloured limelight spilling outside. No canonical Ministry hymns or ballads; the score has free rein.

## 6. Mists on Allomancers
"The mists swirled slightly around the body of a person using any kind of Allomancy." Mistborn are wreathed in curling mists. Spiked beings push the mists away like a weak Steelpush. In book 3 the mists avoid Vin until the earring is torn out, then rush in. The mist spirit is a man-shaped persistent pattern that watches, points, dissipates when approached, and stabs Elend at the Well. Translation: a shader that bends mist particles toward the player while burning, repels them around Inquisitors and koloss, and a silhouette NPC that disperses on contact.

## 7. Existing adaptations
- Mistborn: Birthright (Little Orbit, 2012 to 2017): prequel with Fendin "Fiddle" Fathvell; E3 2012 slice had Fiddle Pulling himself up buildings via fixtures, tossing metal for puzzles, blue line to targets. Cancelled; no official screenshots survive publicly.
- Mistborn Adventure Game (Crafty Games): black-and-white interiors by Ben McSweeney, symbols and maps by Isaac Stewart. Successor Cosmere RPG (Brotherwise) Mistborn books ship September 2026, the first large official colour art corpus for Era 1.
- Cover art: Jon Foster, Chris McGrath, Sam Weber, Marc Simonetti (widely praised), Steve Argyle (leatherbound endpapers), Isaac Stewart (maps, Steel Alphabet).
- Film: Apple TV deal January 2026 with Sanderson writing; first draft July 2026; release realistically 2028 or later. Sanderson is also in talks with AAA studios about a licensed game.
- Fan 2D games: MightyJor's Mistborn Fan Game (GameMaker pixel art), The Children of Preservation (Unity), Project Coinshot (pixel metroidvania, Ori-inspired), Pushing Heights (Era 2). 3D: Invested (Unity, volumetric lines), Ashes of Luthadel (Godot), A Mist Born Fangame (Godot first-person), the Ashes Project concept with pillars "gritty, oppressive, contrast, and stylized reality".

## 8. Maps
Isaac Stewart's Luthadel map follows Victorian maps of London and Paris. Dominances: Central (Luthadel, Fellise, Valtroux, Holstep; plains, scrub, aspen, caves including the Pits and Arguois; Lake Luthadel north, Lake Tyrian west), Northern (Urteau), Eastern (Faleast), Western (Fadrex, Tremredare; eroded plateaus, rock spires, canyons), Southern (Austrex), Crescent, Farmost, Remote, Southern Islands, Terris (snowy mountains, Tathingdwen). The River Channerel runs north to south through Luthadel; Keep Hasting just west of it; the Steel Gate across it to the NW; Kredik Shaw dead centre. Luthadel sits at the magnetic north pole.
