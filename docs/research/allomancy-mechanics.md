# Research: Allomancy, Feruchemy, Hemalurgy as Described in the Books

Supporting research for `docs/DESIGN.md`. Compiled 2026-10-02 from Coppermind (metal, creature, character and location pages), Brandon Sanderson's chapter annotations, the Sanderson FAQ, and 17th Shard physics threads. Where a detail comes from the novels' text rather than a fetched source it is noted.

## 1. The sixteen metals

Four quadrants, each with an external pair and an internal pair; in every pair one metal Pushes and one Pulls; the Pushing metal is usually an alloy of the Pulling one.

| Quadrant | External Pull / Push | Internal Pull / Push |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Iron (Pull metal toward you) / Steel (Push metal away) | Tin (senses) / Pewter (body) |
| Mental | Zinc (Riot emotions) / Brass (Soothe emotions) | Bronze (sense Allomancy) / Copper (hide Allomancy) |
| Temporal | Cadmium (slow-time bubble) / Bendalloy (fast-time bubble) | Gold (your past self) / Electrum (your future shadows) |
| Enhancement | Chromium (drain another's reserves by touch) / Nicrosil (detonate another's metal by touch) | Aluminum (wipe own reserves) / Duralumin (detonate own burning metals) |

God metals: Atium (others' futures) and its alloy Malatium (others' pasts, the "Eleventh Metal").

Sensation: a reserve is felt in the stomach; burning gives a warm feeling, hence "burning". Flaring burns faster for more power and drains the reserve much faster. Metals are drunk as flakes suspended in alcohol; Kelsier's first vial to Vin contains all eight basics. Allomancers burn off leftover metal before sleeping. A mis-alloyed metal causes a blinding headache; a non-Allomantic metal could be deadly.

Burn rates: pewter is the fastest-burning basic metal; copper the slowest. Atium burns very fast (a bead lasts a short fight). A vial of the eight basics covers a night of normal use; flaring eats it in minutes.

What Vin can burn, by book:
- TFE: the eight basics from night one; atium (training and Kredik Shaw); gold (once, ch. 27); malatium (finale); aluminum (fed to her by Inquisitors).
- WoA: duralumin (she finds an aluminum alloy that does not sicken her, first triggered accidentally with tin, causing sensory overload).
- HoA: electrum ("poor man's atium", from the storage-cavern plates). Cadmium, bendalloy, chromium, nicrosil are unknown in Era 1. Treat them as absent.

## 2. Steelpushing and Ironpulling

Blue lines: on burning steel or iron, blue lines connect the Allomancer to nearby metal; line thickness indicates how much metal; coin lines are thread-thin and faint; a keep's iron door frame gives a bold line. They fade with distance, are visible only to the Allomancer, pass through walls, and point at the Allomancer's chest. Skilled users can identify specific metals from the lines. Aluminum shows no line.

Weight rule (Kelsier's lesson): the force acts equally on both ends of the line. Push something heavier than you, or anchored, and you fly; something lighter and it flies; comparable mass and both move. Two Allomancers Pushing on the same anchor: the lighter is thrown (the Keep Hasting Push contest with Zane). Push strength is roughly proportional to the Allomancer's weight but much greater than it, and inversely proportional to distance to the anchor, so a Push off a coin is explosive at launch and fades as you rise until a zenith; a bigger anchor, a flare, or duralumin raises it.

Angles and centre of mass: Pushes go straight along the line from your centre. Push on an anchor not directly below your centre and you are thrown at that angle and tumble; Vin's first attempts spin her. Experts (Kelsier spinning an arrow, Zane rotating while hovering over a coin) can impart spin; treat torque as expert tier.

Core traversal moves:
- Drop a coin, Push: effectively Pushing against the planet. Kelsier flips a coin to the ground and soars to the top of Luthadel's wall; Coinshots crouch before they Push.
- Controlled fall: throw a coin below and Push near the ground to brake. Push too hard and you bounce back up.
- Rooftop travel: toss a coin forward, Push off it as it lands to arc to the next roof; Pull on roof fittings or a spire to swing in. Untrained Coinshots forget anchors behind them.
- Horseshoe trick (WoA 52): cycle three to five horseshoes, Push off one, Pull the one behind up past her, throw it ahead.
- Pulling coins back to the hand to re-use.
- Ironpull danger: Pull something heavier and you fly to it; Pull something lighter and it flies into you. Lurchers use iron defensively, pulling projectiles into a wooden shield.

Coins as weapons: Pushed clips gain speed and punch through flesh; shot horizontally in a flat line. Vin's crowd answer: drop a handful and Push outward in all directions. Steel bubble: Push outward on everything incoming; enemy Coinshots do the same, so coin exchanges become volleys Pushed back and forth. Hazekillers counter with large wooden shields and carry no metal.

Metal in bodies: metal at least partially inside a body cannot be affected by normal Pushes; only the Lord Ruler, or Vin powered by the mists, can overcome that. A spike in an Allomancer's own body can be Pushed by its bearer. This is why coin sprays kill soldiers but not Inquisitors.

Duralumin + steel: an explosion of power; launch incredibly far; move things far too heavy (tossing cavalry by their horseshoes; the Steeljump that bisects Straff). It consumes the entire steel reserve instantly; the user feels torn apart and burns pewter to survive the acceleration.

Aluminum wipes all reserves instantly and is itself un-Pushable.

## 3. Pewter
Increased strength, speed, durability, balance, healing. Strength roughly doubles, triples when flared; balance almost unnaturally smooth; survives otherwise fatal wounds; burns off drugs. Fastest-burning basic metal. The crash: when it runs out after sustained use, a sudden intense fatigue and collapse; masked wounds can kill. Pewter dragging: flaring continuously to run at roughly a galloping horse's speed for a day or more; recovery takes days. Pewter savants lose pain sense.

## 4. Tin
All five senses enhanced at once, no selective control. See through the mists (not total darkness); hear footsteps streets away. Costs: bright light blinds, loud noise deafens, pain scales up. Flaring tin clears a dazed head. Spook as savant: starlight like noon, fights blindfolded, senses the wind of a sword, heartbeats through walls.

## 5. Bronze and Copper
Bronze (Seekers) feels Allomancy as drum beats. Pulses carry length (internal metals longer), direction (Pushing metals wash over, Pulling metals tug), and a quadrant pattern. Copper (Smokers) raises a coppercloud, a pocket of deadened air where pulses cannot be sensed and emotional Allomancy is blocked; copper burns slowest, so Mistborn keep it on. Vin pierces copperclouds because her earring is a Hemalurgic bronze spike. Inquisitors and the Lord Ruler also pierce them. Duralumin + bronze briefly pierces copper.

## 6. Zinc and Brass
Zinc Riots, brass Soothes. Target one person or a group; pick specific emotions; easier to target a direction than an individual. Cannot Soothe yourself; copper immunises a target; extreme emotions interfere. Soothing everything but fear produces panic. The Lord Ruler's city-wide Soothing registers as crushing depression near Kredik Shaw. Duralumin-Soothed targets feel empty; duralumin-Rioted feel an intense flare. Vin uses it in combat to make guards hesitate or panic, and with duralumin seizes control of Hemalurgic creatures.

## 7. Atium, Malatium, Electrum, Gold
Atium beads are reflective, silvery, like drops of liquid, pea-sized. Burning shows a translucent atium shadow of every person and object performing what it will do a second or two ahead; nothing can surprise you. Two atium burners: shadows split into a multitude of possibilities; the fight collapses to skill and who runs out first. Electrum splits your own shadow. Vin's counter against Zane (WoA 47): watch his body react to her future attack, then pick a different one, giving herself two shadows. Rarity: mined only at the Pits, one geode per prisoner per week; Kelsier shatters the crystals. Seers are atium Mistings; the mistfallen are mist-Snapped Seers. Atium is Ruin's body.

Malatium is silvery white like porcelain; shows another person's past or alternate self; the shadow is incorporeal. Gold shows your own alternate self; Vin sees a second Vin and is nauseated.

## 8. Duralumin and Aluminum
Duralumin instantly uses up any metals burning simultaneously, releasing a burst. Per metal: steel/iron huge launch; pewter momentary huge damage/resistance; tin temporarily numb, blind, deaf; bronze loud pulses; zinc/brass total override, control of koloss/kandra; atium deeper future. Aluminum: all reserves vanish; un-Pushable; no blue line.

## 9. Feruchemy
Store attributes in metalminds and tap later; no loss of energy, only a change of time and rate. Storing weakens you by that attribute. Iron = weight (store to fall safely, tap to resist Pushes and hit harder), steel = speed, pewter = strength, tin = one sense per tinmind, zinc = mental speed, brass = warmth, copper = memories, bronze = wakefulness, gold = health, atium = age. Metalminds are keyed to their maker. Compounding: a Feruchemist who is also an Allomancer burns a metalmind for tenfold energy and stores the excess; Rashek compounded atium and gold, surviving decapitation and flaying, with bracers driven through his skin so they cannot be Pushed. Vin, drawing on the mists, rips them free; he ages a thousand years in seconds.

## 10. Hemalurgy
A spike through a bind point steals an attribute and staples it into the recipient, tearing holes in the soul through which Ruin pushes mental pressure. Humans need four spikes for control; kandra two; koloss four. Spikes decay fast outside a body.

Inquisitors: 9 to 11 spikes; two through the eye sockets protruding several inches out the back of the skull (they see via blue lines); a linchpin between the shoulder blades (later Inquisitors plate it over); six to eight in the torso. Powers: steel/iron/pewter/tin, bronze that pierces copper, atium, gold healing. Kill methods: pull the linchpin, behead them, or rip out a critical spike. Removing one eye spike is not enough. Marsh ends with twenty-two spikes. They fight with obsidian axes and flared pewter.

Koloss: blue skin, red eyes, 5 to 12 ft, grow until heart failure; skin tears at about 10 ft; four spikes; two emotional states, boredom and rage; enormous jagged blades their own size. Control: duralumin + brass smashes through their will; the link persists afterwards without metal. Kandra: two spikes in the shoulders (Blessings of Potency, Awareness, Presence, Stability); immune to emotional Allomancy except via duralumin; rebuild on a corpse's bones.

## 11. Mistings, Snapping, savants, mist sickness
Mistings burn one metal (Coinshot, Lurcher, Thug, Tineye, Smoker, Seeker, Soother, Rioter). Era 1 Allomancy needs noble ancestry within five generations and a Snap (traumatic experience; nobles beat their children as a test). Savants form from constant flaring. Mist sickness (HoA): the mists Snap exactly sixteen percent of those exposed; most recover in a day; one sixteenth stay sick sixteen days (mistfallen) and are Seers. The mists swirl toward Allomancers burning metal and shy away from anyone bearing a spike.

## 12. Vin's combat style and set pieces
Kit: two glass (obsidian) daggers, a pouch of clips, vials in a sash, the mistcloak. Mistcloak (Sanderson FAQ): hundreds of long ribbonlike strips sewn together at the shoulders and across the chest, otherwise hanging separate like overlapping streamers; dark grey; strips half an inch to an inch wide; when she moves they twist and curl like the mists; thick on the shoulders but unconstraining; built to tear free if grabbed. Style: small, fast, acrobatic; instinct over plan; Push/Pull for mobility and surprise, coins at range, daggers close, pewter-enhanced koloss swords when needed.

Set pieces: Kredik Shaw (atium, obsidian axe wound, caught by Sazed); the finale (through the stained glass, malatium reveals Rashek, earring ripped out, mists, bracers torn off, speared). WoA: coin volleys and Push contests with Zane; the Assembly coin massacre; the duralumin duel with Zane; horseshoe flight; a storm of death through the koloss, pewter runs dry, she Soothes the army, kills Straff. HoA: herding koloss by emotion; Inquisitors on Kredik Shaw's spires in the rain, swinging an Inquisitor by its head spike until only the head remains; Marsh rips out her earring; the mists pour in; she flattens the palace. Kelsier: Keep Venture (eight hazekillers); the square (bars spinning in air to deflect arrows, Inquisitor beheaded with its own axe, then killed by the Lord Ruler's spear).

Flags: no source confirms Vin mass-Pushing koloss spikes at Luthadel; the canonical resolution there is Soothing. The Fadrex fight with Marsh is not described in detail anywhere accessible; treat details as design licence.

## 13. Numbers
Vin: barely over five feet; no canonical weight (fan estimates 90 to 110 lb). A boxing (gold) = 100 clips (copper). Licensed replicas: clip about 2 cm / 2.7 g, boxing about 3 cm / 15.5 g. Jump distance never quantified; Kelsier clears Luthadel's wall from a standing Push. Koloss 5 to 12 ft. One vial holds all eight basics; count carried unspecified.
