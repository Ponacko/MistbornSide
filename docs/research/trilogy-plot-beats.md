# Research: Trilogy Plot Beats, Cast, Locations

Supporting research for `docs/DESIGN.md`. Compiled 2026-10-02 from Coppermind chapter summaries (via archived snapshots), Coppermind location pages, and Wikipedia. A few details marked "(from memory)" could not be re-verified online.

Notation: **[V]** Vin present / POV. **[V-ABSENT]** Vin not present (cutscene or alternate playable). **ACTION** = fight/chase/stealth. **TALK** = dialogue/politics.

## Book 1: The Final Empire (prologue + 38 chapters + epilogue)

Epigraphs: Alendi's logbook. Readers assume it is the Lord Ruler's journal; the twist is that the Lord Ruler is Rashek, the Terris packman the journal complains about.

### Prologue
Tresting plantation. Kelsier, Mennis, Lord Tresting, an obligator. [V-ABSENT] ACTION. Kelsier talks with skaa at night, then slaughters Tresting, his obligators and soldiers, and burns the manor. Iconic: Kelsier smiling in the mists, scarred arms, burning manor.

## Book 1 heist structure
Hired by Yeden's rebellion. (1) Army: recruit 10,000 skaa via Soothers, train in the Arguois caverns under Ham. (2) House war: Kelsier murders nobles and plants bodies; Vin as Valette plants rumours at balls; House Renoux is the arms-dealing front. (3) Ministry: Marsh infiltrates and becomes an Inquisitor; Clubs' shop hides the crew. (4) Garrison: lure the Luthadel Garrison out. (5) Atium: seize the hoard. (6) Eleventh Metal to kill the Lord Ruler. Kelsier's secret plan: destroy the Pits, become a martyr, stockpile weapons, OreSeur wears his bones. There was no atium hoard.

## Locations
- Luthadel: black ash-stained capital; eight gates named for metals (Steel, Iron, Bronze, Copper, Brass, Zinc, Pewter, Tin); districts (the Twists, Ashwarrens, Sootwarrens, Old Gate); Great House keeps (Venture, Hasting, Elariel, Tekiel, Lekal, Erikeller, etc.) with stained glass and towers; the Garrison; Canton of Inquisition; fountain square; Clubs' shop; Camon's lair.
- Kredik Shaw: Hill of a Thousand Spires; mismatched towers and bare iron rods; domed mural room with the hut-within-a-building replicating Rashek's hovel; throne room ringed by a giant mist stained-glass window; constant Soothing. Built over the Well. Flattened by Vin in HoA 73.
- Fellise: whitewashed granite suburb an hour away; Renoux mansion; bronze-bar spikeway to Luthadel.
- Arguois caverns: rebel training caves.
- Pits of Hathsin: crystal-lined gorges; Terris refugee settlement later; connects to the kandra Homeland; final battle site.
- Terris / Tathingdwen: northern mountains.
- Urteau: wooden buildings; drained canals used as roads ("streetslots", the Marketpit); underground lake in the cache beneath the Canton of Inquisition.
- Fadrex City: red-orange rock-shelf walls; canyon entrance; Keep Orielle; cache beneath the Canton of Resource.
- Vetitan: mining town; Ministry building with trapdoor to the cache.
- Statlin City: second cache.
- Kandra Homeland / Trustwarren: caves adjoining the Pits; circular metal-walled chamber with the Trust.
- Storage caverns: five (Luthadel, Statlin, Urteau, Vetitan, Fadrex), each with supplies and a steel plate.
- Conventical of Seran: windowless steel-plated fortress; Kwaan's plate.

## Epigraphs as framing
TFE: Alendi's logbook (false lead). WoA: Kwaan's plate, corrupted in transcription. HoA: Sazed-as-Harmony. Each book contradicts the last: a natural codex-unlock system.

## Timeline
Collapse in 1022 FE (TFE spans roughly a year). WoA opens a year later and runs autumn to first snow. HoA opens about a year after the Well and ends with the Catacendre in 1025 FE.
